EIS Quick Login (Chrome extension)

Install
1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent (Chrome loads it from here, don't delete it).
2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on "Developer mode" (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the unzipped eis-quick-login folder.
4. Pin the extension from the puzzle-piece menu.

First time
- Click the icon, save your LDAP user id and password.
- Paste your grid card (A-40 B-15 ... H-50) and save. Or skip it: the grid step will ask once.
- Grid card is copiable from grid chrome extension. Use 'copy-all' and paste it directly.

Layout
- Home: LOGIN button on top, quick-open tiles for HOME / PUNCH / SALARY / LEAVE / BIO,
  settings tiles (ACCOUNT, LOOK), and your grid card at the very bottom.
- ACCOUNT and LOOK open as their own pages with a BACK button.
- LOOK has 8 themes (Classic, Mint, Ocean, Bubblegum, Grape, Lime, Mono, Cocoa).
  The toolbar icon (a lightning bolt) recolours to match the theme.

Every time after
- Click the icon, then "LOGIN TO EIS". It opens the login page, fills your login,
  fills the asked grid cells, and lands on the EIS home page.

Looks
- Pick one of 6 colour themes at the bottom of the popup. The on-page panels switch with it.

Storage
- chrome.storage.local on this browser profile. Plain text, not synced, not sent anywhere.
